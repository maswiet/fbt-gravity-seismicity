## Summary

A review (Berita Sedimentologi 45, 2020) of **Indonesian sedimentary-basin outline maps** and **sediment-thickness / depth-to-basement** understanding. Traces the history of basin definitions and compiles depth-to-basement maps for the main producing basins. Directly relevant to this project's **CRUST1.0 sediment correction** (an independent cross-check of sediment thickness) and to naming/interpreting basins on the Moho map.

## Key Points

- **Basin counts differ by author/agency:** Sujanto (1997) 60 basins; BPMIGAS/LAPI-ITB (2008) 86 Tertiary basins; **Badan Geologi / Geological Agency (2009) 128 basins**. Status classes: producing (~14–17), discovery, no-discovery, undrilled (~22–42); split into Western vs Eastern Indonesia.
- **Sediment thickness maps:** Hardy et al. (1997) "Total Sediment Thickness Map of the Indonesian Region" (0–9000 m); updated Indogeo/Darman map (Fig. 12). These use well tops + **gravity anomaly** + outcrop control.
- **Badan Geologi (2009) map is derived from GRAVITY + geological data** — a direct precedent for gravity-based basin/basement mapping in Indonesia.
- **Depth-to-basement per basin:** North Sumatra up to ~8 km (>5 s TWT; Jawa/Lhok Sukon deeps); Central Sumatra >2 km (Duri/Minas/Kotabatak); South Sumatra / Central Palembang 4–7 km (>4 s TWT); NW Java ~4 km; East Java >3 km (Madura, inverted wrench zone). Eastern Indonesia (except Salawati) has poor well control — basement extent from outcrops.
- Sediment thickness controls petroleum system; basement highs/deeps organize fields.

## Relevance to this project

- **Cross-check for the sediment correction** ([[wiki/concepts/gravity-data-corrections]], CRUST1.0): our CRUST1.0-derived sediment thickness over Indonesia was 0–8.7 km — consistent with Hardy (1997)/Darman (0–9 km) and per-basin depths here.
- **Basin inventory & names** for interpreting the Moho map and the fore-arc idea ([[wiki/questions/applying-spherical-gravity-inversion-to-indonesia]]): e.g. Sumatra fore-arc (Sibolga/Bengkulu), Bintuni, Salawati, Kutei.
- Supports the finding that offshore/eastern basins lack constraints — echoing our land-biased seismic validation limitation.
