## Summary

The **official, current (2022) Sedimentary Basin Map of Indonesia** published by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM), updated from Badan Geologi (2020). A single 1:5,000,000 sheet mapping **128 named sedimentary basins** with their **areas** and a **tectonic-setting classification**. This is the definitive basin inventory for the project — for naming/interpreting features on the Moho map, for the fore-arc basement idea, and as the authoritative basis for any Indonesia-specific sediment correction.

## Key Points

- **128 basins**, each named with area (km²) — e.g. North Sumatra 134,196; Sibolga 86,626; Central Sumatra 115,416; South Sumatra 144,619; Kutai 130,970; Salawati 15,329; Bintuni 39,727.
- **Tectonic-setting classification (crucial):**
  - A = back-arc (busur belakang), B = intermontane, **C = fore-arc (busur depan)**, D = trench (palung), E = foreland (daratan muka), F = passive margin, G = oceanic, H = deltaic, I = rifting valley, J = transtensional (strike-slip), K = transtensional marginal-oceanic.
- **Status classes:** producing (with production wells), discovery, prospective (petroleum-system/seismic/well data), unexplored (with/without limited data).
- WGS84; datasources include Badan Geologi, LEMIGAS, wells, seismic, and gravity.
